## 1. Brand Identity & Mission

Rhombus is a cloud-managed physical security platform founded by cybersecurity experts. The company's mission is to make the world safer with simple, smart, and powerful physical security solutions.

Rhombus consolidates cameras, access control, sensors, alarm monitoring, and AI analytics into a single customizable platform. From setup to management and maintenance, Rhombus streamlines the entire physical security experience so organizations can see, manage, and respond to critical events in real-time.

### Brand Attributes

All design and writing should express these six core attributes:

- **Simplicity** — Clean, uncluttered design
- **Cleanliness** — Polished, professional presentation
- **Modernity** — Forward-looking, current aesthetics
- **Intelligence** — Data-driven, smart technology
- **Approachability** — Friendly, accessible communication
- **Innovation** — Cutting-edge, pioneering solutions

## 2. Logo System

### 2.1 Logo Composition

The full Rhombus logo is comprised of:
- **Alpha Camera logomark** (the diamond/rhombus icon) in the default gradient lockup
- **Logotype** set in a custom, rounded **New Hero Semi Bold** font

### 2.2 Configurations

| Configuration | Usage |
|---------------|-------|
| **Horizontal** | Preferred / default — use whenever possible |
| **Vertical (stacked)** | Available when horizontal is not optimal |

### 2.3 Clearspace

Clearspace for the full logo and the logomark should be **equal to 0.5x of the logomark**. Always maintain this minimum clearspace around the logo.

### 2.4 Color Variants

| Variant | When to Use |
|---------|-------------|
| **Gradient** (default) | Always preferred on web and mobile applications |
| **White / Monochrome** | Situations where the gradient has low contrast |
| **Duotone** | Print applications where gradient may not be suitable (available upon request) |
| **Black** | Low-contrast light backgrounds |

Contact marketing@rhombus.com with questions about which logo variant to use.

### 2.5 Logo Misuse Rules — 10 DO NOTs

1. **DO NOT** change the distance between the logomark and logotype
2. **DO NOT** alter the direction of the gradient on the logomark
3. **DO NOT** distort the logo in any way, shape, or form — maintain original lockup ratios
4. **DO NOT** add any effects (drop shadows, blurs, glows, etc.)
5. **DO NOT** change any of the colors of the logo lockups
6. **DO NOT** retype the logotype or change the font — use logos as provided
7. **DO NOT** change colors to brand colors — use provided lockups only
8. **DO NOT** rotate the logomark in any way
9. **DO NOT** change the alignment of the logomark and logotype
10. **DO NOT** use the logomark as a letter in any way or scenario

## 3. Rhombus Shape Design Elements

Key design elements derived from the Rhombus logo that reinforce brand identity:

- **Rhombus shape** — The diamond form used as a framing/masking device
- **Diagonals** — Angular lines echoing the rhombus geometry
- **Rounded corners** — Softened edges reflecting approachability
- **Masks** — Using the rhombus shape to frame imagery or content

These elements build visual recognition and make assets unmistakably Rhombus.

## 4. Product Naming System

For product-specific logos within the Rhombus suite:

- Product name font: **Sora Light**
- Product name size: **0.5x the size of the logomark**
- Position: to the right of the horizontal logo
- The cap height of the product name equals the x-height of the logotype
- **Always use horizontal configuration** — never stack product names

### 6.5 Color Usage Rules

- **Black text**: Always use NEUTRAL-8 (`#0B0C0D`) — never pure black (`#000000`)
- **White text**: Always use White / NEUTRAL-1 (`#FFFFFF`)
- **Headers & important graphics**: Use TEAL-5 (`#17323B`)
- **Pops of color** (banners, accents): Use TEAL-2 (`#4BEBFF`) and TEAL-3 (`#006F94`)
- **Majority of asset backgrounds**: NEUTRAL-1 and NEUTRAL-2
- **Overall balance**: Colors should feel harmonious and well-proportioned
- **Accessibility**: Be mindful of accessibility when using colors — pair text colors accordingly for sufficient contrast

## 7. Typography

### 7.1 Primary Fonts

| Role | Font | Weight |
|------|------|--------|
| **Hero** | Sora | SemiBold |
| **H1** (titles & subtitles) | Sora | Medium |
| **H2** | DM Sans | SemiBold |
| **Body** | DM Sans | Regular |

### 7.2 Fallback Fonts

| Font | Context |
|------|---------|
| **Verdana** | Secondary font, ideal for PowerPoint decks and emails through HubSpot |
| **Helvetica** | Final fallback when Sora, DM Sans, and Verdana are unavailable |

### 7.3 Console & Alerts Font

| Font | Context |
|------|---------|
| **Nunito Sans** | Used on the Rhombus console — use in marketing materials **only** for alerts and console mockups |

### 7.4 Application Contexts

Sora and DM Sans are the preferred fonts across all brand touchpoints:

- One pagers
- Brochures
- Tradeshow banners
- Presentation decks
- Website pages
- Business cards
- RFPs
- Social media graphics

### 7.5 Type Hierarchy

Maintain strong hierarchy in type at all times. Custom specimens may exist for specific collateral, but the fundamental hierarchy (Hero > H1 > H2 > Body) should always be respected.

Contact marketing@rhombus.com with typography questions.

## 8. Custom Icons

- Icons communicate functions and features in a graphic style
- Use icons to break up large sections of text and communicate bullet points
- **Style**: Flat, minimal, and tech-forward — conveys expertise and precision
- **On dark backgrounds**: Teal-filled circles with white icon outlines
- **On light backgrounds**: Teal outline icons (no fill)
- Partners may source their own icons as long as they align with the flat, minimal, tech-forward style

Contact marketing@rhombus.com with questions about icon sourcing.
